YOSHA VOICE AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

YOSHA VOICE AI converts text supplied by users into audio using the ElevenLabs text-to-speech service.

Information processed

  • Text and voice settings: Text entered by you, the selected voice, model and speech settings are sent securely to our application server to fulfil your audio-generation request.
  • Third-party processing: The text and selected voice settings are forwarded to ElevenLabs to generate speech. Their processing is subject to the ElevenLabs Privacy Policy.
  • Generation history: The current version stores a small list of recent generation details in the browser/app's local storage on your device. It is not an account-based cloud history. Clearing app data or local storage removes this history.
  • Audio: Generated audio is returned to your device for playback and download. The app does not provide a cloud audio library.
  • Technical data: Our hosting provider may process standard server logs, such as IP address, request time and error information, to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

We use submitted text and settings only to provide text-to-speech generation, troubleshoot failures and protect the service from abuse. Do not submit confidential, sensitive or personal information unless you have the right to do so.

Data sharing and retention

Text-to-speech requests are shared with ElevenLabs because it provides the voice-generation engine. Server logs are retained according to the hosting provider's operational policies. Local history remains on your device until you clear it or remove app data. We do not sell personal information.

Children

YOSHA VOICE AI is not designed specifically for children. Do not use the service in violation of applicable age requirements.

Security

Requests to our hosted API use HTTPS. The ElevenLabs API credential is kept on the server and is not embedded in the Android application. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can avoid submitting text, clear the recent-generation history in the app, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data. For privacy questions, contact the YOSHA VOICE AI publisher through the contact details on the Google Play listing.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.
